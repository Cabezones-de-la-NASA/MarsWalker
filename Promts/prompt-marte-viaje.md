# Comenzar viaje

## Prompt

> con esa pagina crea una nueva versión, pero sin las tarjetas, sin las lunas solo marte rotando con una frase en medio que diga "Comenzar viaje". Con un fondo negro y las estrellas

## Ajuste posterior

> Puedes resaltar un poco más las estrellas y que el planeta este un poco más grande 

## Tipografía y brillo de las estrellas

> Cambia la tipografía y que las estrellas se vean un poco más luminosas

## Astronauta en primer plano

> Puedes crear una cabeza de astronauta hiperealista  al enfrente de todo, colocarla en la esquina de la parte inferior izquierda, y el planeta de marte de fondo

### Prompt de generación del casco

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Generate one isolated photorealistic astronaut helmet and its attached compact neck seal, as a production foreground cutout for a beautiful interactive website with planet Mars on black behind it. Frame the complete helmet within a square canvas with very small breathing room, a large helmet viewed at three-quarter profile, turned to the upper RIGHT (toward Mars in the center of the webpage). Close-up aerospace photography of a real worn off-white EVA helmet with realistic materials (ivory polymer, brushed aluminum ring, rubber gaskets, minimal intricate hardware), sealed large dark smoked bronze visor with restrained warm copper highlights, tiny realistic visor scuffs. The person inside is hidden by the dark visor. Light the top and right side with softly directional warm orange illumination, subtle cool bounce on the left; retain convincing darker shadows. High-end documentary space photography. The lower neck seal is short, gently tucked under the helmet. This asset will be anchored at the bottom left; avoid shoulders and torso. Genuinely transparent background with a clean alpha silhouette; no backdrop, no stars, no floor, no cast shadow outside the silhouette, no planet rendered in the asset, no words, no brands or insignia. Just the realistically photographed helmet and neck seal.

## Casco frontal y luz de la escena

> Que la cabeza sea más grande y de frente, aparte ponlo un poco más pequeño y un poco más a la derecha, agrega efectos de iluminación profesionales

Para esta versión se interpretó «un poco más pequeño y un poco más a la derecha» como un ajuste a Marte.

### Prompt para la vista frontal

> Edit the reference astronaut helmet cutout into the SAME helmet photographed from directly in front, at 0° yaw and 0° roll, straight-on eye-level camera, centered smoked visor, both ear hardware mounts visible and the shell geometrically balanced about the vertical centerline. Camera sees the full helmet and short neck seal in an almost edge-to-edge square frame. Preserve the hyperreal worn ivory EVA helmet, the visible fine wear and rubber and metal hardware, and the opaque dark bronze visor that hides the wearer. Relight with a cinematic split: rich but restrained amber-orange edge and reflected light on the right/top-right from a Mars backdrop that will be composited separately, subtle desaturated steel-blue rim and fill on the left, deep clean shadows and controlled specular highlights across the curved visor. Add a very subtle soft copper reflection that follows the visor's curvature, not a distinct second planet image. Premium aerospace photography, straight front portrait, centered visor. Preserve transparent background with genuine alpha. Single isolated helmet, no torso or shoulders, no text or logos, no backdrop, no stars, no floor, no drop shadow outside the helmet. Do not retain the reference's three-quarter profile: it must face the camera directly.

## Encuadre

> La cabeza más grande y el planeta más de lado

Se amplió el casco frontal y se llevó el planeta más cerca del borde derecho.
